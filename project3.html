Senior Capstone — Metal Powder Aerosolizer

Research, Mechanical Design & Testing

Problem

Our client, Professor Ozan Özdemir of Northeastern's Cold Spray Lab, needed a metal powder aerosolizer that could consistently aerosolize fine particles (0.5–10 microns) with controlled volume flow rates and particle concentrations.

Action

  • –

    Researched how fine metal powders are typically aerosolized in industry and academia, then brainstormed approaches. We narrowed it down to two: a rotary brush method and a novel vacuum method our group developed.

  • –

    Built a prototype for each approach and placed both on a load cell to determine which provided a more consistent feed rate.

Image of our Rotary Brush Prototype.

Image of our Vacuum Method Prototype.

  • –

    We placed both prototypes on a load cell and collected data to determine if either provided a constant feed rate. The rotary brush data was indecipherable; the vacuum method showed a clear exponential trend — making it the clear choice to pursue.

Load Cell Data for the Rotary Brush Approach.

Load Cell Data for the Vacuum Method.

  • –

    Developed a final prototype designed to fit inside the existing pressure vessel. We applied vibration to linearize the exponential trend in feed rate, successfully achieving the controlled, consistent aerosolization our client needed.

Outcome

The final prototype achieved consistent, controlled aerosolization of fine metal particles — fulfilling the client's requirements. Load cell data confirmed the vibration method successfully linearized the feed rate.

2022 World Cup Predictor

Python & Machine Learning

Problem

For an Intro to Machine Learning course, my group was tasked with selecting a project of our choice, finding and cleaning the data, then applying machine learning techniques from class. We chose to predict the outcome of the 2022 FIFA World Cup using historical World Cup data and socioeconomic indicators.

Action

  • –

    Scraped match data from a historical World Cup database and socioeconomic data from Macrotrends. Cleaned both datasets and merged into a single dataframe.

  • –

    Tried a decision tree and K-nearest neighbors classifier. Accuracy was poor due to difficulty predicting draws, so we removed draws from the dataset.

  • –

    Used a random forest classifier to identify the 10 most important features out of 42, then re-ran KNN using only those.

Our Feature Importance based on the Random Forest calculations.

  • –

    Cross-validated the KNN model and found 16 to be the optimal number of neighbors. Built a confusion matrix confirming an accuracy score of approximately 0.75.

  • –

    Applied the final model to all 2022 group stage matchups and continued bracket-style until we had a predicted winner.

Confusion Matrix depicting how often the KNN classifier accurately predicts wins and losses.

Outcome

The model produced a full predicted bracket for the 2022 World Cup with a KNN accuracy score of ~0.75. We didn't get the winner right — but Netherlands came close!

Predicted Bracket of the 2022 World Cup. We got it wrong but Netherlands got close!
